About Us

Gojira is a band founded in Bayonne, France, in 1996, and is widely regarded as one of the most influential forces in modern Progressive Death Metal.

Their music masterfully combines technical riffing, complex rhythms, and heavy atmosphere. However, the core of Gojira's artistry lies in its lyrical themes, which explore deep environmental consciousness, spirituality, and the mystical aspects of nature.
Every album offers listeners not just a crushing metal experience, but also a powerful philosophical journey, compelling them to reflect on the planet's future and our place within the universe. Gojira continues to push musical boundaries while delivering their potent message.
